# USA Market Page

## Goal
Create a complete USA-to-India FMCG market page at `/global-presence/usa`, matching the supplied premium dark reference while staying consistent with the existing Rishishwar website.

## What will be built
- Replace the current generic USA market view with a USA-specific page.
- Build a strong opening section focused on bringing USA FMCG products into India, with two clear actions: explore the opportunity and contact the team.
- Add a compact benefits strip for market planning, import/compliance support, and distributor/retail access.
- Add a “Why India” section using carefully worded, sourced market context rather than unsupported claims.
- Add product-category cards for personal care, home care, packaged food and beverages, oral care, and baby care.
- Add a four-step support journey: research, compliance preparation, distributor/retail access, and launch support.
- Add a final market-assessment call to action and retain the current official source links/disclaimer.

## Images and visual direction
- Treat the tall page screenshot as the design reference, not as a page asset.
- Use the supplied USA–India trade scene as the main page image and the supplied category photographs in the appropriate cards.
- Keep the current Royal Corporate Cinema palette, Archivo headings, Barlow body text, dark photographic sections, and existing site header/footer.
- Use deliberate image crops for mobile, tablet, and desktop so products and landmarks remain visible.

## Responsive behavior
- Mobile: single-column reading order, full-width actions, horizontally safe category layout, and compact spacing.
- Tablet: two-column category grid and balanced opening content.
- Desktop: immersive opening image, dense category grid, and wide step-by-step process without text collisions.
